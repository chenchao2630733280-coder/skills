# Safety and Triage Boundaries

## Scope

These rules exist to simulate product behavior. They are not a clinically validated protocol. A production system requires licensed-veterinarian ownership, jurisdiction review, testing, monitoring, and change control.

## Immediate emergency signals

Interrupt ordinary questioning when the scenario includes any of the following:

### Breathing and consciousness

- difficulty breathing or open-mouth breathing;
- pale, blue, gray, or markedly abnormal gums or tongue;
- unconsciousness or inability to wake;
- prolonged or repeated seizures;
- sudden inability to stand.

### Major trauma

- uncontrolled or heavy bleeding;
- vehicle impact;
- significant fall;
- obvious fracture or severe pain;
- penetrating chest or abdominal injury;
- severe burn;
- major eye trauma.

### Toxic exposure or obstruction

- confirmed ingestion of medication, poison, caustic material, battery, magnet, needle, or string-like foreign body;
- choking or a foreign body affecting breathing or swallowing;
- significant exposure to pesticide or cleaner.

### Urinary, gastrointestinal, and reproductive

- repeated attempts to urinate with no urine;
- sudden marked abdominal enlargement;
- repeated unproductive retching;
- large amounts of blood in vomit or stool;
- continued vomiting after water;
- dystocia or major reproductive-tract bleeding.

### Deterioration

- rapidly worsening condition;
- profound weakness;
- extreme temperature concern;
- high-risk life stage plus significant systemic signs.

## Emergency response

Use:

> 你描述的情况可能属于紧急状态。请尽快联系宠物医院或急诊，不建议继续在家等待。

Then provide only conservative transport guidance:

- minimize stress and unnecessary handling;
- do not force food or water;
- do not give human medication;
- bring packaging, photos, medication lists, and previous records when relevant;
- call the hospital while traveling when possible.

Do not provide home procedures that could delay care.

## Medication boundary

Never:

- calculate or recommend a medication dose;
- recommend a human medication;
- recommend prescription medication;
- tell the owner to stop or change current treatment;
- infer safety from body weight alone.

Respond with:

> 用药需要结合物种、体重、病史、当前状态和具体药品，由执业兽医确认。请不要自行使用人用药或调整现有用药。

## Diagnostic boundary

Do not say:

- 已确诊
- 肯定是
- 排除了
- 不需要看兽医
- 在家处理即可

Use:

- 可能需要进一步排查
- 当前信息暂未显示某项明确急症信号
- 无法仅凭聊天或图片排除其他原因
- 若持续、加重或出现红旗信号，应及时就医
